# Aerial Survey Services Agreement — TEMPLATE DRAFT

> **Draft template, not legal advice.** Self-drafted agreements are defensible for
> the first clients; have a Canadian lawyer review this before the second or third
> paying client. Bracketed items are completed per engagement.

**Between** [legal business name] ("the Operator") **and** [client legal name]
("the Client").
**Site:** [address and boundary description, with a map attached as Schedule A]
**Effective:** [date]

## 1. Services

The Operator will fly the Site by drone and produce the deliverables listed in
Schedule B.

| Deliverable | Description |
|---|---|
| Orthomosaic | A top-down map of the Site, delivered as a web link and a GeoTIFF file |
| 3D Reconstruction | An explorable photographic 3D model of the Site, delivered as a web link |

**Visits:** [one visit] / [every [interval], at approximately [time of day], for
[term]].

## 2. Accuracy

Deliverables are **visual-grade**. Relative geometry is sound and appearance is
finished, but no absolute positional accuracy is stated or warranted. The
deliverables are not a legal survey, are not produced by a licensed land
surveyor, and must not be relied on for boundary determination, engineering
design, quantity calculations used for payment, or any purpose requiring
measured accuracy.

## 3. Site access and permission

The Client confirms that it owns the Site or has authority to permit flights over
it, and grants permission to take off from, land on, and fly over the Site on the
agreed dates.

Full coverage of a Site boundary may require flight over or imaging of adjoining
land. The Client will tell the Operator of any adjoining land where overflight is
not permitted, and the Operator will plan around it.

## 4. Flight conditions

- **The Site must not be worked during flights.** The Client will ensure no
  workers, contractors or visitors are present in the flight area at the agreed
  times. The Operator's aircraft may not legally be flown within 5 metres of, or
  over, people who are not part of the flight crew.
- The Operator may postpone, shorten or abort a flight for weather, light,
  airspace restrictions, the presence of people, or any safety reason, without
  penalty. A postponed visit is rescheduled at no extra charge.
- The Operator holds [a Transport Canada Pilot Certificate – Advanced
  Operations, number [ ]] and the aircraft is registered as [ ].

## 5. Privacy and notice

The Client will notify its personnel and post notice at the Site entrance on
flight days, using wording supplied by the Operator.

Imagery is handled under the Operator's Privacy and Retention Policy, provided
with this agreement. In summary, raw imagery and derived deliverables are
retained for 12 months from each flight date and then deleted. **If the Client
needs deliverables kept longer, the Client must download and keep its own copy.**

## 6. Ownership and licence

The Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use the delivered
deliverables for its own business purposes, including sharing them with its
consultants, lenders and prospective buyers.

The Operator retains ownership of raw imagery, processing methods and software,
and may use deliverables in its portfolio [only with the Client's written
consent] / [unless the Client opts out in writing].

Viewers delivered with the deliverables include third-party open-source software,
provided under its own licence terms; the notices are included with each
delivery.

## 7. Delivery and confidentiality

Deliverables are delivered at unlisted web links. **Unlisted links limit discovery
but are not access control**: anyone who obtains a link can view it. The Client
should treat links as confidential and tell the Operator before the engagement if
the Site requires stronger protection.

The Operator will not disclose Client deliverables to third parties except as
required by law.
