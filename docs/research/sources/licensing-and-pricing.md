A 30-day trial and a "contact us" path for custom terms, >25 seats, or Epic Pro
Support are also offered.

### FAQ — "Is the $1 million threshold tied to product revenue or company revenue?"

> There are two $1 million thresholds, and they depend on what you make and how
> much you make. It also depends on whether you're using just RealityScan or
> combining it with Unreal Engine and Twinmotion. You can find more information
> in the Unreal Engine FAQ.
>
> RealityScan Seat prices are determined by your annual gross company revenue.
> If your company has reported earnings of $1 million or more in the last 12
> months or fiscal year, you'll need to pay for seats.

### FAQ — "Is there an offline installer available for RealityScan?"

> Yes, an offline installer is available for subscribers from the Epic Developer
> Portal, facilitating distribution without the Epic Games launcher. Educational
> institutions needing to install RealityScan across labs can also access an
> offline installer by request.

Note the words "for subscribers". Whether a free-tier user can obtain the Linux
`.deb`/`.rpm` without the Epic Games Launcher is not stated here.

### Why `realityscan.com/en-US/linux` returns 403

It is not a network block. The page redirects to an Epic gate reading **"Epic
Account Required — The page you are trying to reach requires an Epic account."**
The download is account-gated; the EULA and the licensing page above are not.
